# 04 — Training and nutrition framework (as practised, Sept 2026)

## Training
- **Lifting**: alternating A/B split, 2×/week, ~45 min. Day A = kettlebell/unilateral, posterior chain (KB RDL, chest-supported row, double-KB front squat, half-kneeling press, suitcase carry, dead bug). Day B = barbell/machines/bar (weighted pull-ups, incline DB press, hack squat/leg press, ham curl, overhead triceps, incline curl, face pulls, dead hang). Full program in `reference/training_program_posture_density.md`.
- **Current mode**: maintenance. After the 2026-08-17 rebound (+7–10 % across all lifts in one session, read as expressed latent strength after exiting a year-long deficit), loads are held; rep schemes rotate every 8–12 weeks at matched RPE ≤ 8; no progression is targeted. Maintenance evidence: ~1/3 of building volume at maintained intensity preserves strength and muscle; 2 sessions/week is the floor at this age.
- **Aerobic**: Zone 2 run and swim as primary modalities (swim ~2,000 m freestyle sessions, pace improving toward ~2:09/100 m; a spontaneous 39 km run exists in history). Calisthenics/mobility secondary and may share a day with Z2 (calisthenics first).
- **Deload**: monthly; volume ~50 %, load 75–80 %, normal tempo (slow tempo generates fatigue and is not used).
- **Planned, gated**: Norwegian 4×4 intervals — only after sleep improves; HRV state is the readiness gate.
- **Contrast therapy**: sauna / cold immersion in use; timing relative to lifting sessions is a live experiment (cold immersion immediately post-lift blunts hypertrophy signalling; keep ≥ 4–6 h separation or place on non-lift days).
- **Known response patterns**: > 120 min training days → next-day HRV/RHR dip; no same-day run + calisthenics history (no personal data yet).

## Nutrition
- **Protein floor**: ≥ 1.9 g/kg/day (≈ 137 g at 72 kg), hard rule in every phase.
- **Calorie cycling** toward lift days; weekly total at the phase target.
- **Anchors**: Subway chicken bowl, double protein (reliable lunch). Refeed default after fasts: protein-forward, low-fat, cooked (lamb + sweet potato worked; whole pizza + consecutive high-fat meals did not).
- **Supplements**: creatine monohydrate 5 g/day (continued through fasts; expect creatinine elevation on labs), omega-3 ethyl esters with food (not fasted), magnesium glycinate pre-bed, whey protein. Skin: CeraVe Hydrating Cleanser + Moisturizing Cream; Rosiver evenings; Nizoral routine; chlorine mitigation for swimming (silicone cap, chelating shampoo 1–2×/week).
- **Logging**: food logging is dead since 2023 and is **not being rebuilt**. The 7-day weight trend against the tripwire is the energy audit. Log only deviations and anchors as events (fast, refeed, travel, alcohol, protein-floor misses). Logged days in 2026, when they existed, averaged ~2,600 kcal against a 2,000 kcal target — the leakage hypothesis stands until the trend says otherwise.
- **Fasting**: see `01_protocol_state.md` — monthly 36 h with electrolytes; 72 h only trigger-based.
