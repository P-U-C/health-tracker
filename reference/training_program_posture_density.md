POSTURE + DENSITY The Alternating A/B Program for the 40+ Lifter
"No conditioning cosplay. No excessive warm-ups. No bullshit."
Two complementary training days you can alternate forever. Day A delivers pure kettlebell work for posterior chain
dominance. Day B adds machines, free weights, and pull-up bar for frame development. Each session: 45 minutes.

## Page 2


### DAY A — PURE KETTLEBELL "Posture + Density" — ~45 minutes


### WARM-UP (5 min max)

KB Halos ×10 ea direction | Hip Hinge ×10 | Dead Hang 30s | Goblet
Squat ×1 set

### BLOCK 1 — HINGE + UPPER BACK Posture Priority

- **A1. Heavy KB Romanian Deadlift — 4×8**
    - 2-sec stretch at bottom, squeeze glutes hard at top
    - Hip-width stance, push hips BACK (not down)
    - KB stays close to legs throughout
    - Stop when you feel hamstring stretch
Rest: 45-60s
- **A2. KB Chest-Supported Row — 4×10**
    - Elbows 30-45° — target mid-back, NOT traps
    - Lie chest-down on incline bench
    - Pull to ribcage, squeeze shoulder blades
Rest: 45s
This pairing alone will pull your shoulders back over time.

### BLOCK 2 — SQUAT + OVERHEAD STABILITY

- **B1. Double KB Front Squat — 4×6-8**
    - Clean KBs to rack position, elbows high
    - Slow 3-sec descent, upright torso
    - Full depth — hips below parallel
Rest: 60s
- **B2. Half-Kneeling KB Press — 4×6-8/side**
    - Squeeze back glute HARD
    - Ribs down — don't let them flare
    - Press straight up, not forward
Rest: 45s
Half-kneeling presses are money for posture + shoulder health.

### BLOCK 3 — CARRIES + CORE THICKNESS

- **C1. Heavy KB Suitcase Carry — 3 rounds/side**
    - 30-40 second walk each side
    - Stay TALL — ear over shoulder over hip
    - Do NOT lean away from weight
Rest: Minimal between sides
- **C2. Dead Bug or Weighted Sit-Up — 3×10-12**
    - Lower back stays FLAT against floor
    - Controlled movement, not fast
    - Core braced throughout

KB Clean → Squat → Press — 2 rounds ×

## Page 4


### DAY B — WEIGHTS / MACHINES / BAR "Frame + Shape" — ~45 minutes


### WARM-UP (5 min)

Scap Pull-Ups ×5 | Face Pulls ×15 | Light
Incline Press ×10

### BLOCK 1 — BACK WIDTH + UPPER CHEST

- **A1. Pull-Ups (weighted if possible) — 5×4-6**
    - Dead hang EVERY rep — full extension
    - Initiate by depressing shoulder blades
    - Control the descent — no dropping
Rest: 60s
Can't do 4-6? Use a band or slow negatives.
- **A2. Incline Dumbbell Press — 4×6-8**
    - 30-45° bench angle
    - 3-second eccentric (lowering)
    - Full stretch at bottom
Rest: 60s
This block fixes the "compressed" torso look.

### BLOCK 2 — LEGS (HEAVY BUT EFFICIENT)

- **B1. Hack Squat / Leg Press / Front Squat — 4×8**
    - Choose based on equipment
    - Full depth, controlled descent
    - Drive through full foot
Rest: 60-75s
- **B2. Seated or Lying Ham Curl — 3×10-12**
    - Squeeze hard at peak
    - Slow negative 2-3 sec
    - Full ROM, no momentum
Rest: 45s

### BLOCK 3 — ARM DENSITY Short + Brutal

- **C1. Overhead Triceps Extension — 3×10**
    - Cable or dumbbell
    - Full stretch overhead
    - Keep elbows stable
- **C2. Incline DB Curl — 3×10**
    - 45° bench for max stretch
    - Full stretch at bottom
    - No swing — strict
Rest: 30-45s between supersets

### BLOCK 4 — POSTURE FINISHER MANDATORY

- **D1. Face Pulls or Rear Delt Fly — 3×15**
    - Slow and strict
    - Pull to eye level+
    - External rotation at end
- **D2. Dead Hang — 2×45-60 sec**
    - Relax shoulders — decompress
    - Breathe deeply and slowly
    - Overhand grip, shoulder width

### FORM GUIDE + WEEKLY STRUCTURE

KB RDL — The Foundation
Setup: Hip-width stance, soft knees. Move: Push hips BACK (not down) — it's a hinge, not a squat. KB stays close to
legs. Stop when hamstrings stretch. Return: Drive through whole foot, squeeze glutes HARD at top.
Pull-Ups — Dead Hang Required
Start: Complete dead hang, arms fully extended. Pull: Depress shoulder blades first, then pull. Chin over bar, chest
toward bar. Lower: Control every inch. Each rep starts and ends in dead hang.
Suitcase Carry — Anti-Lateral Flexion
Key: Do NOT lean away from the weight. Stay vertical — ear over shoulder over hip. Your obliques should be fighting to
keep you upright. That's the point.
Half-Kneeling Press — Posture Builder
Position: Back knee under hip. Secret: Squeeze back glute HARD — this prevents back arch. Ribs down, core braced.
Press straight UP, not forward.
